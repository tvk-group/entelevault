# Security-disclosure and bug-bounty readiness

This gate verifies governance for a private disclosure channel, public bug bounty, or vendor-coordinated program. It does not receive vulnerability reports, exploits, targets, proof-of-concept material, or researcher identities, and it cannot authorize testing.

## Required controls

Eligibility requires all fourteen controls: published policy, exact scope registry, safe harbor, authorized channels, researcher privacy, triage SLA, severity method, duplicate handling, reward policy, legal escalation, emergency contact, evidence handling, remediation tracking, and public-disclosure approval.

Policy gaps, scope ambiguities, overdue triage, privacy breaches, and unresolved disputes must be zero. Every one of the 16,384 control combinations is evaluated; only the fully satisfied combination can proceed to separate human program approval.

## Boundary

Inputs contain only classifications, a revision, aggregate counts, and SHA-256 digests. Reports, findings, exploit code, proof-of-concept payloads, targets, researcher identities, credentials, and wallet material are rejected. The decision cannot activate a program, scan or exploit, publish a disclosure, pay a reward, remediate, deploy, sign, or move assets.
