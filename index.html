How It Works

From sensitive data to controlled, verifiable access.

EnteleVAULT follows a privacy-first flow: encrypt, bind, authorize, seal, and audit.

Step 01

Encrypt

Private records, documents, credentials, and identity data are stored in encrypted vault structures.

Step 02

Bind

Records can be linked to wallets, verified identities, roles, devices, or authorized accounts.

Step 03

Authorize

Access is controlled through permissions, policies, presence checks, wallet signatures, and risk context.

Step 04

Audit

Access events and updates are timestamped, sealed, indexed, and traceable for accountability.
